Privacy Policy – Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool I use to upload and schedule videos on my own YouTube channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses. With my permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to my channel (the youtube.upload permission). It does not read, collect or store data about other people.

Where data is kept. The sign-in token stays on my own computer. Nothing is sent to any server except Google's.

Sharing. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Google policies. Shorts Uploader uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
