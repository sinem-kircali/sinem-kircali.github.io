01 — Why data

I solve actual jigsaw puzzles for fun — slowly, without shortcuts (more on that in the Hobbies section below 🧩). What draws me in isn't just finding the right piece. It's figuring out why everything else doesn't fit.

That's also what draws me to data. I enjoy questioning assumptions, finding patterns that aren't immediately obvious, and making sense of things that initially seem disconnected. There's something really satisfying about finally seeing how everything fits together.

And that's how I've always thought about choosing a career: don't just find a job you like. Find something you love doing, and build a career around it. For me, data was exactly that.

02 — About

My path here

Open to work

Data Analyst / Data Scientist

Europe

I'm a data scientist and analytics professional based in Rotterdam. As of 1 November 2026, I've completed my MSc in Data Science & Marketing Analytics at Erasmus University Rotterdam.

My path into data science wasn't exactly a straight line, but each step brought me closer to the kind of work I wanted to do. I started with a double major in Industrial Engineering and Economics at Koç University, alongside a minor in Business Administration. Engineering taught me to structure complex problems and think in systems, while economics helped me connect analytical thinking to real business decisions.

During my internship at Mondelēz International and my role at L'Oréal, I discovered how much I enjoyed working with real consumer and e-commerce data — not just analyzing numbers, but finding insights that could actually influence decisions.

After graduating, I joined PwC as a Business Analyst in Application Development, managing projects from requirements gathering and solution design through testing and delivery. It gave me a strong understanding of how business problems become technical solutions, but it also helped me realize something: I didn't just want to define what needed to be built. I wanted to be the person building the analytical solutions myself.

That realization led me to pursue a more technical Master's. Alongside my studies, I spent six months as a Data Engineering Intern at Bynder, building automation solutions and working hands-on with Python, SQL, and cloud infrastructure. At the same time, my Master's thesis allowed me to explore another side of data science: using machine learning, statistical inference, and topic modeling to understand how delivery performance shapes customer satisfaction.

Now that I've completed both my internship and my Master's, I'm looking to bring these experiences together. What motivates me most is working through messy, real-world problems, understanding what's happening beneath the surface, and turning that understanding into something useful.

I didn't choose data science because it was the obvious next step. I chose it because, through every role I've had, I kept finding myself drawn to the same thing: solving problems through data.
